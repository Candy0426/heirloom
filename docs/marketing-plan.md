## 1. Executive Summary

**Heirloom** is a zero-knowledge digital inheritance vault for couples and families. Our mission is to ensure that the people you love can access your digital assets, financial information, and important documents if something happens to you.

**Marketing Goal:** Generate 1,000 beta signups in the first 6 months with a conversion rate of 15% from waitlist to active users.

## 2. Brand Positioning

### 2.1 Value Proposition

**For couples** who want to protect their family's future, **Heirloom** is a **secure, zero-knowledge digital vault** that ensures assets and documents are passed on if something happens. Unlike traditional estate planning, Heirloom is **automatic, encrypted, and private** — even we can't access your data.

## 3. Target Audience

### 3.1 Primary Persona: "Protective Parent Maria"
- **Age:** 30-45
- **Status:** Married, 1-2 children, dual-income household
- **Location:** EU (Greece, Germany, Italy)
- **Income:** €3,000-6,000/month
- **Pain Points:**
  - Worries about what happens to assets if something happens to her/husband
  - Doesn't have a formal will or inheritance plan
  - Has crypto assets, multiple bank accounts, insurance policies
  - Doesn't trust banks/lawyers with sensitive info
- **Motivations:** Peace of mind, protecting family, simplicity
- **Tech Savvy:** Moderate-high (uses banking apps, crypto wallets)

### 3.2 Secondary Persona: "Crypto Investor Alex"
- **Age:** 25-40
- **Status:** Single or partnered, no children yet
- **Location:** Global (remote worker, nomad)
- **Income:** Variable, €2,000-10,000/month
- **Pain Points:**
  - Has crypto wallets with significant holdings
  - No way for family to access if he loses keys or dies
  - Distrusts centralized custody
  - Wants self-sovereign solution
- **Motivations:** Self-custody, decentralization, security
- **Tech Savvy:** Very high

### 3.3 Tertiary Persona: "Prepared Partner Elena"
- **Age:** 45-60
- **Status:** Married 20+ years, adult children
- **Location:** EU suburbs/small cities
- **Income:** €4,000+/month, mortgage almost paid
- **Pain Points:**
  - Has accumulated assets but no digital inventory
  - Partner doesn't know all account details
  - Traditional estate planning is expensive and slow
- **Motivations:** Simplification, legacy, avoiding family disputes

## 5. Marketing Strategies

### 5.1 Content Marketing
**Goal:** Build trust through education about digital inheritance, crypto estate planning, and zero-knowledge security.

**Content Pillars:**
1. **Education:** "What happens to your crypto when you die?"
2. **How-To:** "How to create a digital inventory of your assets"
3. **Stories:** "How one family's bitcoin was saved"
4. **Security:** "What is Shamir's Secret Sharing?"

**Formats:**
- Blog posts (2x/week)
- Short videos for TikTok/Reels/Shorts
- Infographics for Instagram/Pinterest
- Newsletter (monthly)

### 5.2 Community Building
**Goal:** Build a community around digital legacy and security.

**Channels:**
- **Discord/Telegram:** User community, AMAs, support
- **Reddit:** r/personalfinance, r/cryptocurrency, r/privacy
- **LinkedIn:** Professional audience, corporate partnerships
- **Twitter (X):** Thought leadership, industry insights

### 5.3 SEO / Organic Growth
**Goal:** Rank for high-intent keywords.

**Target Keywords:**
| Keyword | Volume | Difficulty |
|---------|--------|------------|
| digital inheritance | Medium | Low-Med |
| crypto estate planning | Low | Low |
| what happens to bitcoin when you die | Low | Low |
| digital asset protection | Medium | Med |
| time-locked cryptography | Low | Very Low |
| zero-knowledge vault | Low | Very Low |
| inheritance planning Europe | Low | Low |

### 5.4 Influencer / Partnership Marketing
**Goal:** Leverage trusted voices in finance, security, and crypto.

**Target Partners:**
- Personal finance YouTubers (EU-focused)
- Crypto educators
- Estate planning lawyers
- Security/privacy advocates
- Couples/lifestyle bloggers

### 5.5 Paid Advertising (Phase 2)
**Goal:** Scale once product-market fit is confirmed.

**Channels:**
- **Google Ads:** Search ads for high-intent keywords
- **Meta (FB/IG):** Retargeting, lookalike audiences
- **Twitter Ads:** Crypto audience targeting
- **YouTube:** Pre-roll ads on finance/security videos
